What is this catalogue?

{{ params.nom_outil }} brings together, in one place, the doctoral theses and master’s theses of its partner higher-education institutions.

Each institution keeps cataloguing its work in its own tool (Zotero, library catalogue…). Records arrive here automatically, so what you see is the record kept up to date by the institution’s library.

The platform is a catalogue: it describes each work and, when it is online, links to the full text deposited by the institution.

Searching for a document

Type one or more words in the search bar on the home page, then click Search. The search covers:

  • title, author and supervisor;
  • keywords, abstract and field;
  • institution, doctoral school or faculty;
  • national number.

A few tips:

  • The more words you type, the more precise the results: irrigation rice Senegal finds works about all three.
  • Accents and capital letters do not matter, and small typing mistakes are tolerated.
  • To find a work whose national number you know, type it in full, without spaces.
  • To browse the whole catalogue, click Theses or Master’s theses in the menu without typing anything.

Titles and abstracts are published in the language in which the works were written, often French: searching with words in that language gives the best results.

Refining with filters

To the left of the results (the Refine button on a phone), filters let you narrow the list:

  • Institution and Doctoral school / Faculty;
  • Type: doctoral thesis or master’s thesis;
  • Status: defended or in progress;
  • Field, Year and Language.

The number next to each value shows how many documents match it. You can tick several values and combine filters with a keyword search.

Active filters appear above the results: click one to remove it, or click Clear all to start again.

Sorting and browsing results

The Sort by menu offers:

  • Relevance: documents that best match your words first (a word found in the title counts more than one found in the abstract);
  • Year, newest or oldest first;
  • Title A–Z;
  • Recently added, to see the latest records.

Choose how many results to show per page and move between pages at the bottom of the list.

The page address includes your search and filters: you can copy it, bookmark it or send it to a colleague, who will see the same list.

Reading a document record

Click a title to open its record: author, supervision, jury (when provided), institution, year, abstract, keywords and national number.

The access box tells you how to read the text:

  • View the document: the full text is online. The button opens the institution’s repository in a new tab.
  • Restricted access: the institution has chosen not to publish the text online (confidentiality, embargo…). Contact its library to consult it.
  • Not available online: the work is listed, but the text has not been deposited yet.

From the record, the Explore links lead to other works from the same institution, the same doctoral school or faculty, or the same year.

The national number

Every defended work receives a unique 15-character national number, so that it can be cited and found without ambiguity. For example:

SN UC T 2024 0042 76

  • SN: national prefix (Senegal);
  • UC: institution code;
  • T: doctoral thesis (M for a master’s thesis);
  • 2024: year;
  • 0042: sequence number within the institution for that year;
  • 76: check key, which detects a typing mistake in the number.

The number is written without spaces (SNUCT2024004276); the spaces above are only there for readability. It is assigned at the defence: a work in progress does not have one yet. It never changes and is never reused.

Citing and exporting

One document: on its record, the Cite this document box gives the reference in APA style, with a button to copy it. You can also download it in BibTeX (LaTeX, Overleaf) or RIS (Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote) format.

A list: above the results, the Export button downloads the list on screen, filters included, as a spreadsheet (CSV, for Excel or LibreOffice), in BibTeX or in RIS.

Frequently asked questions

I cannot find a work I know about.

Try fewer words, or remove the filters. If the work still does not appear, its institution may not have catalogued it yet: contact the library concerned.

A record contains a mistake.

Records are maintained by the institutions. Report the mistake to the institution’s library: once it has been corrected in their catalogue, the correction appears here at the next synchronisation, usually within the hour.

How do I deposit my thesis?

Deposits are made with your institution’s library, following its procedures. Once your work has been catalogued by the institution, it appears on the platform automatically.

A page says “This document has been withdrawn from the catalogue”.

The institution has withdrawn the record. Its national number is still shown and is never given to another work. To find out more, contact the institution’s library.

Can I use the platform in another language?

Yes, when several languages are offered: choose FR, EN or PT at the top of the page. The interface changes language; titles and abstracts stay in the language in which the works were written.
